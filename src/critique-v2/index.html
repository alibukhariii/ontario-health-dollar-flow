Why “Primary health care” is smaller than the physician line

The Primary health care purpose contains published team and organization programs classified to that row. Physician billings and other insured services remain in “Physician, practitioner and other insured services,” including payments that may support primary care. The two rows should not be read as total primary-care spending versus non-primary-care spending.

Method and evidence

How the page was assembled

The source is Ontario's 2025-26 Detailed Schedule of Payments. Each positive line is assigned to exactly one analytical purpose using its ministry, payment category and exact published program detail, never the recipient's name. Official program labels remain unchanged beneath that purpose.

The current release applies reviewed identity-link decisions. Aliases are combined only where authoritative evidence supports the match. Unresolved labels remain separate. People are removed from the public recipient list while their aggregate amount remains in the total.

Operation of Hospitals combines base, targeted, activity-based and one-time funding. A consistent recipient-level split among HBAM, QBP and related mechanisms is not publicly available, so those mechanisms are not invented as amount-bearing branches.
