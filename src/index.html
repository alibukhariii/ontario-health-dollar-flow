Ontario health-ministry published payments

Ontario’s Ministry of Health and Ministry of Long-Term Care paid $97.4B* across the positive payment entries published for 2025–26.

$65.2B is listed across 2,079 named payees†.

$32.251B (33.1%) is accounted for as program-level aggregate payments, including Ontario Drug Benefit and physician/practitioner services, but is not itemized to named recipients.
