Who received Ontario’s health dollars?

$97.4B in positive published payments; $65.2B went to 2,081 named recipients and $32.3B was not itemized.

$97.0B net after a −$421.0M Ontario Government Pharmacy adjustment. The adjustment is excluded from the forward flow because it is a signed reconciliation rather than a positive recipient payment.

Source: Ontario Public Accounts 2025–26 Detailed Schedules of Payments
