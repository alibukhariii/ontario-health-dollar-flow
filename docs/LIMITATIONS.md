# Limitations

- The main total is the sum of positive payment lines in the Detailed Schedule of Payments, not all Ontario health-system expense.
- The $32.251 billion not itemized to named recipients includes program-level aggregates and grouped payments. It is not evidence of a complete recipient list, and the product does not infer named recipients for these amounts.
- Public Accounts payments to Ontario Health, Ontario Health atHome and Accerta do not provide a complete downstream payment crosswalk. Ontario Health and OHaH audited statements are separate reporting views; the difference between their totals and ministry payee rows is not a reconciled amount.
- The $3.607 billion OHaH contracted-care expense is not itemized by contracted provider in the collected statements. Accerta’s split between onward payments and its own costs is not published in the collected evidence.
- Some payee identities remain unresolved or are retained as source labels. The project does not merge labels on similarity alone.
- A profile unavailable in this release means no reviewed profile is connected here. It does not mean the organization has no financial statements.
- Ontario Health’s audited transfer expenses and Public Accounts payee amounts are overlapping reporting views. No public recipient-by-recipient crosswalk reconciles them completely.
- Published payment labels, legal organizations and financial-statement reporting entities are different units. The pipeline combines labels only with reviewed authoritative evidence.
- `Operation Of Hospitals` does not publish a consistent recipient-level split among global base, HBAM, Quality-Based Procedures, targeted programs and one-time funding.
- Recipient financial statements describe the whole reporting organization. They do not show which ministry payment paid for which expense.
- Corporation-level financial amounts are not allocated to hospital sites.
- Missing evidence is not treated as zero, a performance problem or a disclosure failure.
- The product does not rank efficiency, quality, outcomes, patient complexity, need or value for money.
- Intermediary receipts and independently reported outflows are separate views and must not be summed.
- The current release covers 2025–26 only. A later 2024–25 release will use the same contracts after this release is accepted.
