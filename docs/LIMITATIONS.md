# Limitations

- The main total is the sum of positive payment lines in the Detailed Schedule of Payments, not all Ontario health-system expense.
- Disclosure thresholds and aggregate program lines mean 33.10% of the positive total is not itemized to a public recipient.
- A payment to Ontario Health or another intermediary does not identify the ultimate provider unless a separate compatible amount is public.
- Published payment labels, legal organizations and financial-statement reporting entities are different units. The pipeline combines labels only with reviewed authoritative evidence.
- `Operation Of Hospitals` does not publish a consistent recipient-level split among global base, HBAM, Quality-Based Procedures, targeted programs and one-time funding.
- Recipient financial statements describe the whole reporting organization. They do not show which ministry payment paid for which expense.
- Corporation-level financial amounts are not allocated to hospital sites.
- Missing evidence is not treated as zero, a performance problem or a disclosure failure.
- The product does not rank efficiency, quality, outcomes, patient complexity, need or value for money.
- Intermediary receipts and independently reported outflows are separate views and must not be summed.
- The current release covers 2025–26 only. A later 2024–25 release will use the same contracts after this release is accepted.
