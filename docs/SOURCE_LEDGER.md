## Evidence stops

The release uses the following controlled public labels:

- `Not itemized by recipient`
- `Downstream amount not published`
- `2025–26 financial statement unavailable`
- `Legal identity not resolved`
- `Source scope incompatible`
- `Restricted from publication`
- `Financial profile not collected in the current scope`

Lake of the Woods District Hospital is the one included hospital corporation without an available 2025–26 statement. North of Superior Healthcare Group’s revenue and expense observations are retained, but its unreconciled final-result subsection is excluded.
