# Organization identity review

Published labels are not merged merely because they look similar. Each approved alias requires authoritative evidence for the legal organization, while unresolved labels remain separate.

## Decisions in the 2025–26 release

| Published labels reviewed | Decision | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `St Thomas-Elgin General Hospital`; `The St. Thomas-Elgin General Hospital` | Combined as St. Thomas Elgin General Hospital | The hospital’s [audited financial statements](https://www.stegh.on.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/2019-03-31-STEGH-FS.pdf) identify “The St. Thomas Elgin General Hospital” as the incorporated hospital; its [official site](https://www.stegh.on.ca/about-us/) uses the same institutional name. |
| `Shoppers Drug Mart Inc`; `Shoppers Drug Mart Inc.` | Combined as Shoppers Drug Mart Inc. | Loblaw’s [official privacy policy](https://www.loblaw.ca/en/loblaw-privacy-policy/) identifies Shoppers Drug Mart Inc. as a covered legal company. The two source labels differ only by terminal punctuation. |
| `Shoppers Drugmart Specialty Health Network` | Kept separate | Loblaw identifies [Shoppers Drug Mart Specialty Health Network Inc.](https://www.loblaw.ca/en/loblaw-privacy-policy/) as a separate subsidiary. |
| `Google Canada Corporation`; `Google Canada Corporation*` | Kept separate pending source clarification | Google’s [official Canadian store terms](https://store.google.com/intl/en_ca/about/device-terms/) confirm Google Canada Corporation, but neither Google nor the Public Accounts metadata explains whether the asterisk is only a footnote marker. Similarity alone is insufficient to merge the rows. |

After these decisions, 2,084 published aliases resolve to 2,079 payees, including 2,076 non-intermediary organizations. Of the alias rows, 1,936 remain based on the published label only. Those rows are not presented as verified legal-entity matches.

The overview’s **2,079 named payees** includes three intermediary payee labels. The recipient ranking contains 2,076 non-intermediary organizations; intermediary payees appear in a separate diagram layer and are not ranked as final recipients.

`Ihf` is retained as a separate published payee with **identity unresolved**. It is not matched to Ontario Health or another organization. The source-native payee name and source row remain in `route-to-source-lineage.csv`.

For all reviewed merges, the canonical ranking name is shown with the published source name(s) and recorded match evidence in recipient details. The lineage export retains each original published payee label, payment-line ID and source row number so a merge does not erase the source record.
